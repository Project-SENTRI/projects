# Project SENTRI Projects
The Projects repository contains initiatives developed under Project SENTRI to promote awareness, education, and exploration of AI safety and responsible AI.
Projects may include awareness campaigns, educational resources, technical demonstrations, and collaborative initiatives.
The goal of these projects is to make AI safety concepts more accessible and encourage broader participation in conversations surrounding the future of artificial intelligence.

---

## Categories
- Awareness Initiatives
- Educational Projects
- Technical Demonstrations
- Collaborations
---

## Project Principles
Projects developed under Project SENTRI focus on:
- **Accessibility** — Making AI safety understandable for diverse audiences.
- **Education** — Encouraging informed learning and discussion.
- **Responsibility** — Promoting thoughtful and safe engagement with AI.
- **Openness** — Sharing knowledge and encouraging collaboration.
